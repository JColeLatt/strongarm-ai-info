# Strong Arm Technologies — Business Information

**Veteran-Owned Technology Support in Shelby, North Carolina**

Strong Arm Technologies provides practical computer repair, diagnostics, data recovery assessments, file transfers, backup assistance, VHS-to-digital conversion, photograph scanning and everyday technology support. We work with customers in Shelby, NC, and surrounding communities. Services are arranged by appointment.

**Computer Repair & Upgrades**

Troubleshooting slow computers, startup problems, software issues and potential hardware upgrades, including SSD installations.

**Data Recovery & Backups**

Assessment of lost files and help planning backups. Whether recovery is possible depends on the device and the cause of failure.

**Preserving Family Memories**

VHS and home-video conversion, plus printed-photo scanning, help families keep and share important memories.

**Everyday Technology Help**

Assistance with printers, Wi-Fi, setting up devices, moving files and remote computer support when appropriate.

**How to Get Started**

Use our Customer Request form to describe what you need. A request starts a conversation; it does not book an appointment or authorize paid work. We will discuss availability, service options and cost before work is approved.
